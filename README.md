The operating system for Zaal's Instagram, [@zaal.eth](https://www.instagram.com/zaal.eth/). One post a day, Stories daily, Live every week once the account is allowed to. Everything that goes out starts in this repo.

## The system in one page

1. **Fix the front door today.** Bio: one line on who Zaal is and what he builds, one link (zaonexus or the Sparkz page). Pin three posts: one Zaal talking, one ZAO moment, one music moment. Stop reposting other people's reels into the grid; share those to Stories instead.
2. **One reel a day, from footage we already have.** Twitch streams, BCZ YapZ, fractal calls, ohnahji appearances, ZAOstock, the Zoroastrian trip. Clips are 7 to 15 seconds for a moment, 30 to 90 seconds for a teaching. Never over 3 minutes: Instagram does not recommend longer reels to non-followers.
3. **Stories every day.** One to three. Behind the scenes, polls, reshares of friends, the countdown to anything live.
4. **One carousel a week.** A teaching in 5 to 8 slides: Web3 101, what a fractal is, ZAO numbers, a Zoroastrian teaching.
5. **Live weekly once unlocked.** Same day, same time every week. Schedule it in the app a day before so followers get the 24 hour and 15 minute reminders. Save every replay as a reel.
6. **Measure shares and saves, not likes.** The 2026 ranking order is watch time, then sends per reach, then likes. Reply to every comment in the first hour.

Details: [docs/playbook.md](docs/playbook.md). First 30 days: [docs/30-days.md](docs/30-days.md). How a clip gets made and posted: [docs/pipeline.md](docs/pipeline.md).
