The system

  1. Fix the front door. One-line bio, one link, three pinned posts. Shares of other people's reels go to Stories, not the grid.
  2. One reel a day from footage that already exists: Twitch, BCZ YapZ, fractals, ohnahji, ZAOstock, the Zoroastrian trip. Under 90 seconds, hook in the first 3.
  3. Stories every day, one to three.
  4. One carousel a week, a teaching in 5 to 8 slides.
  5. Live weekly once unlocked, same day and time, scheduled a day ahead, replay saved as a reel.
  6. Measure shares and saves, answer every comment in the first hour.
