# Playbook: what works on Instagram in late 2026

Pulled together 11 Nov 2026 from current creator and agency write-ups. None of this is official Instagram documentation except the Live rule, which Meta confirmed to TechCrunch on 2 Aug 2025.

## The rules that shape the plan

| Rule | What it means for zaal.eth |
|---|---|
| Live needs a public account with 1,000+ followers | 137 today. Live is locked. Target one: 1,000. |
| Reels over 3 minutes are not recommended to non-followers | Keep every reel under 3 minutes. Clips 7 to 15 s, teachings 30 to 90 s. |
| Ranking: watch time and completion first, then sends per reach, then likes per reach | Make things people send to a friend. A clip that makes one person send it beats ten likes. |
| Hook in the first 3 seconds or reach dies | Start on the line, not on the intro. Caption on screen from frame one. |
| Connected reach (followers) and unconnected reach (discovery) are ranked separately | Reels are for discovery, Stories are for the people already here. Do both. |
| Caption SEO: the first line is searchable | Put the subject in plain words in line one ("Zoroastrian priest explains..."). 3 to 5 hashtags, not 30. |
| Other platforms' watermarks are demoted | Export clean from the editor. |
| Comment activity in the first hour counts | Post when Zaal can answer for an hour. |
| Scheduled Lives send reminders at 24 h, 15 min and start | Once unlocked, schedule every Live a day before. |
| Live Rooms let 3 guests join | One Live with Feliq or an artist reaches both follower bases. |

## Frequency

- Reels: 1 a day is the ask from Zaal. The write-ups say 3 to 5 a week is where quality holds; if a week is thin, 4 good ones beat 7 weak ones. Never 3 a day.
- Stories: every day, 1 to 3.
- Carousels: 1 a week. Carousels get saves, and saves rank.
- Live: weekly, same day and time, once unlocked. Replay saved as a reel.

## The road to 1,000

No date is promised. What moves it:

1. Reels with a send-to-a-friend moment, daily.
2. Collabs: the Collab post feature puts one reel on two profiles. Feliq, ohnahji, WaveWarZ artists, MetaMu, N3M. Every collab is new eyes.
3. Cross-send: every reel also goes to Farcaster and the newsletter with "follow on IG for the daily". Not to The ZAO's X while that is paused.
4. Guest on other people's Lives until our own unlocks. ohnahji's stream already does this.

## Formats that fit each pillar

| Pillar | Format |

| Music battles | 7 to 15 s battle moment, artist tagged, Collab post with the artist |
| YapZ | best 30 s of the episode, both hosts tagged |
| Zaal | phone video, raw, Stories first, reel if it lands |
